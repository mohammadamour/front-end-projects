Frequently asked questions

Tell me about your customer support?

Our dedicated support team is available 24/7 across multiple channels including live chat, email, and phone. We guarantee a response within 2 hours during business hours, and our customer satisfaction rate consistently exceeds 97%.

What does your reporting look like?

We provide comprehensive monthly reports with custom dashboards covering key performance indicators, campaign analytics, ROI tracking, and actionable insights tailored to your franchise objectives.

What does training look like?

We offer onboarding workshops, recorded video tutorials, and ongoing live training sessions. Every franchisee receives a personalized training plan and access to our self-service knowledge base with over 200 articles and guides.

What type of agencies do you work with?

We work with franchise brands across retail, food & beverage, fitness, home services, and professional services. Our platform is built to scale from 10 to 10,000+ locations, adapting to each brand's unique operational needs.

Is there any on-going training?

Absolutely. We run quarterly webinars on new features, monthly office hours for Q&A, and maintain an always-updated resource library. As the platform evolves, your team always stays current.

What if my clients need technical help?

Frankly, it's the best in the business and we'll back that up with references from our clients. Our support moves at the speed of your business. Our Trainers and operations teams offer support in multiple time zones.
